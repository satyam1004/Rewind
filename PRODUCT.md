# Rewind
## Register
product
## Platform
web
## Users
A personal music collector keeping owned audio from the 1980s, 1990s and 2000s, listening on laptop, tablet and phone.
## Product Purpose
Upload, organize, play and download a persistent personal collection. Audio bytes and metadata are stored together in a user-configured MySQL database. No remote music catalog or fake playable content.
## Brand Personality
Nostalgic, tactile, unhurried. A record shelf beside a sunlit desk, with a softly lit stereo for evening listening. Light content with deep green navigation provides readable browsing in either setting.
## Design Principles
A user's own audio is the content. Player access persists while browsing. Filters never interrupt playback. Mobile composition is designed independently of desktop density. Reasonable defaults and design decisions delegated in the brief.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard controls, visible focus, labeled fields and player controls, reduced motion, touch targets and responsive layout.

Personal favorites and listening progress stay in each browser profile. A paused resume prompt and editable Up next queue support returning listeners. Visitors get friendly availability messages; admin login reveals configuration details.

Guests receive one shared free preview song. Listener self-registration unlocks the full collection; listener accounts never grant upload or management privileges.

Signed-in favorites and listening history now belong to each account in MySQL. Most played ranks only that account’s activity. The catalog is shared; locally saved resume and queue state are separated per account. This supersedes earlier browser-shared listener favorites.
